SOP — Step by step

Use this guide the first 2–3 times. After that the calculator above does the work — you just need to know what numbers to pull.

Step 1 — Open the client's creative tracker

  1. Go to the client's folder (/Case Source/, /VAM/, /CEO Lawyer/, etc.).
  2. Open the most recent [CLIENT] - Creative Tracker.xlsx (sort by date modified).
  3. You'll use two tabs: Static Creative Tracker and Video Creative Tracker.

Step 2 — Count "launched" statics

  1. On the Static Creative Tracker tab, look at column Status (usually column E).
  2. Launched = rows where Status is any of: Winner, Loser, Testing, On the fence, Past Winner - Fatigued.
  3. Skip rows where Status = Not Launched or is blank.
=COUNTIFS(E:E,"Winner") + COUNTIFS(E:E,"Loser") + COUNTIFS(E:E,"Testing") + COUNTIFS(E:E,"On the fence") + COUNTIFS(E:E,"Past Winner - Fatigued")

Step 3 — Count static winners

  1. Winners = rows where Status = Winner OR Past Winner - Fatigued.
=COUNTIFS(E:E,"Winner") + COUNTIFS(E:E,"Past Winner - Fatigued")

Step 4 — Sum static winner lifetime spend

  1. Column Meta (usually column O) is lifetime ad spend per creative.
  2. Sum the Meta spend only for rows where Status = Winner or Past Winner - Fatigued.
=SUMIFS(O:O, E:E, "Winner") + SUMIFS(O:O, E:E, "Past Winner - Fatigued")

Step 5 — Repeat for videos

Switch to the Video Creative Tracker tab and do the same three counts. Column letters are the same on both tabs.

Step 6 — Get the scaling ladder

  1. Ask the media buyer for: current daily spend, target daily spend, end date.
  2. Count days from today to the end date → Days in window.

Step 7 — Plug everything into the calculator above ⬆️

The calculator does Steps 8–11 of the SOP automatically. Read the "Batches per week" headline and the flag banners below it.

Step 9 — Log it

Click "Copy summary for Slack" on the calculator and paste it into the pod channel. Also drop the row into the master log spreadsheet so we track every account every week.

The formulas (Damien's CIS calculator, distilled)

Win rates

Static Win Rate = Static Winners ÷ Statics Launched Video Win Rate = Video Winners ÷ Videos Launched Blended Win Rate = (Static Winners + Video Winners) ÷ (Statics Launched + Videos Launched)

Avg lifetime spend per winner

Avg Spend = (Σ Static winner spend + Σ Video winner spend) ÷ (Static Winners + Video Winners)

Spend over the scaling window

Total Spend = ((Current Daily + Target Daily) ÷ 2) × Days In Window ← linear ladder Spend Gap = Total Spend − (Current Daily × Days In Window) ← incremental spend only

Winners → creatives → batches

Winners Needed = (Spend Gap ÷ Avg Spend) × (1 + safety margin) Video Winners = Winners Needed × Video Mix % Static Winners = Winners Needed × Static Mix % Videos To Produce = Video Winners ÷ Video Win Rate Statics To Produce = Static Winners ÷ Static Win Rate Static Batches/Week = Statics To Produce ÷ (Days ÷ 7) ÷ Statics per batch Video Batches/Week = Videos To Produce ÷ (Days ÷ 7) ÷ Videos per batch Total Batches/Week = Static Batches/Week + Video Batches/Week
Two levers, everything else is noise. Damien's video is explicit about this. If win rate is below 10% or avg lifetime spend per winner is below $20K, the volume number explodes. Fix the lever first. Doubling win rate halves the creative you need. Pushing avg winner spend from $8K to $30K cuts it by ~75%.

At current metrics

Winners needed = ($320K ÷ $8,204) × 1.2≈ 47
Videos to produce = (47 × 0.7) ÷ 0.075≈ 440
Statics to produce = (47 × 0.3) ÷ 0.099≈ 141
Batches per week≈ 47 ❌

At Damien target metrics (15% win rate, $30K spend/winner)

Winners needed = ($320K ÷ $30K) × 1.2≈ 13
Creatives needed = 13 ÷ 0.15≈ 87
Batches per week≈ 5 ✅
Report to pod: "Case Source needs 5 batches/week (3 video + 2 static) assuming media buying gets winner lifetime spend up to $30K. At current $8K lifetime spend, the math demands 47 batches/week — not producible. The bottleneck is media buying, not creative."
